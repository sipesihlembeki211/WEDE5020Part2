##PROJECT OVERVIEW Sipesihle Tutoring Academy "Empowering Students Through Knowledge, Innovation & Excellence"
Organisation Name: Sipesihle Tutoring Academy
Sipesihle Academy is educational tutoring academy that provides academic support to high school and university student. The academy focuses on helping students develop their knowledge, confidence and academic performance through structured tutoring and learning support.
The academy offers support in key academic areas, including STEM, Accounting and Economics.

#Website Goal and Objective 
The main goal for the Sipesihle Tutoring Academy website is to provide students and parents with clear information about academy and make it easy for potential students to enquire about tutoring.
WEBSITE OBJECTIVE
The website will:
•	Introduce Sipesihle Tutoring Academy to potential students.
•	Explain the tutoring service offered.
•	Provide information about subjects and programmes.
•	Allow students to make enquiries.
•	Provide contact information.
•	Provide the academy’s location using a map
•	Make it easy for visitors to navigate between pages.
•	Present the academy as professionals and trustworthy.
